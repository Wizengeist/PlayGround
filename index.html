THE RULEBOOK

HOW TO FIGHT

1 · WIN THE MATCH

Knock your opponent's stamina to 0 before they do the same to you. Win three matches to take the belt.

2 · SPEND MOMENTUM

You start each turn with 3 momentum. Cards cost 0–3. Unspent momentum is lost, so empty your tank.

3 · BUILD HEAT

Taunts and big moves raise your Heat. At 55–60 heat your finisher unlocks — it spends the heat.

4 · CHAIN CARDS

Every card played after the first in a turn adds +1 damage to that card (up to +3). End with your biggest move.

5 · READ THE INTENT

The INCOMING panel shows exactly what your opponent will do next. Bank guard or play a counter.

6 · WORK THE CROWD

Dealing damage fills the crowd meter. Fill it completely for +1 momentum next turn.
